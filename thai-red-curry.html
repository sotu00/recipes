Thai Red Curry (Chicken and Tofu)

One coconut curry sauce split into two pots: chicken in one, tofu and chickpeas in the other. Dairy-free, no peppers, with garlicky roasted green beans.

Serves 4 servings
Prep 10 min
Cook 25 min
Total 35 min

Ingredients

Sauce, for both pots

  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 small onion, sliced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 3 tbsp red curry paste
  • 2 cans (13.5 oz) coconut milk
  • 1 cup vegetable broth
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce

Vegetables, for both pots

  • 2 zucchini, cut in half-moons
  • 8 oz mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 cups snap peas or broccoli florets
  • 5 oz baby spinach
  • 1 lime
  • fresh basil or cilantro

Meat pot

  • 1 lb chicken breast or thigh, thinly sliced
  • 2 tsp fish sauce

Vegetarian pot

  • 1 block (14 to 16 oz) extra-firm tofu, cubed
  • 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained

Green beans

  • 1 lb green beans
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, sliced
  • salt

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Toss the green beans with oil and salt on a sheet pan.
  2. In a large pot, sauté the onion in oil for 3 minutes. Add the minced garlic, ginger, and curry paste and stir for 1 minute.
  3. Add the coconut milk, broth, and soy sauce. Bring to a simmer, then pour half into a second pot.
  4. Add the chicken to one pot and the tofu and chickpeas to the other. Simmer both for 8 minutes.
  5. Roast the green beans for 12 to 15 minutes, adding the sliced garlic for the last 5.
  6. Divide the zucchini, mushrooms, and snap peas between the pots. Simmer 5 to 6 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through.
  7. Stir in the spinach and lime juice. Add fish sauce to the chicken pot only, and a little extra soy sauce to the other if it needs salt.
  8. Serve as is, or over cauliflower rice or a small scoop of brown rice.

Check the curry paste label: some brands contain shrimp paste. Thai Kitchen does not.
